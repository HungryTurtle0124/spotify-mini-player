Connect your own Spotify Premium account. This preview uses a Spotify developer app you configure once; future visits connect automatically.

1. Choose your Spotify app

Create an app in the Spotify Developer Dashboard, or use a Client ID supplied by its owner if they have added your account to their test users. A shared development app supports only a small group of approved accounts.

Use the public Client ID. This extension never needs your client secret. Changing apps signs out the previous connection.

Open Spotify Developer Dashboard ↗

2. Add your redirect URL

In the chosen app’s settings, add this exact address under Redirect URIs, click Add, then Save. This address belongs to your installed extension and may differ from someone else’s.

3. Connect your account

Once the Client ID and redirect address are saved, sign in below. Spotify will open a temporary authorization window. Each installation stores its own sign-in locally.

Sign-in is saved on this computer and renewed automatically, including after Chrome restarts or extension reloads. Disconnecting clears local credentials; app access can also be removed in your Spotify account. Unofficial project, not affiliated with Spotify. Privacy details
